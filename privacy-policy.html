Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2025

Simple Summary: Your data never leaves your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your information. Period.

1. Data Collection

We collect ZERO data.

Reportify does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, reports, images, or any other data on external servers. All data you create within the app remains exclusively on your device.

2. Data Storage

All data created in Reportify is stored locally on your iOS device using:

  • Apple's Core Data framework (for report information)
  • Local file storage (for images)

This data is stored in your device's private app container and is not accessible to other apps or to us, the developers.

3. No Server Communication

Reportify is a 100% offline application. It does not:

  • Connect to any external servers
  • Send data over the internet
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Require an internet connection to function

4. No User Accounts

Reportify does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or register in any way. You can use the app immediately after installation without providing any personal information.

5. Camera and Photo Library Access

Reportify requests access to your camera and photo library solely to allow you to add images to your reports. These images are:

  • Stored locally on your device
  • Never uploaded to any server
  • Only accessible within the app

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app.

6. PDF Exports

When you generate a PDF report, the file is created locally on your device. You have full control over what you do with the PDF (share it, delete it, etc.). We have no access to or knowledge of these exports.

7. Data Backup

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, your app data may be included in your iCloud backup. This is handled entirely by Apple and subject to Apple's privacy policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

8. Developer Access

The developer has ZERO access to your data.

Because all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted anywhere, we (the developers) have no way to access, view, or retrieve any information you create in the app.

9. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any data, our app is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Given the nature of our app (no data collection), we do not anticipate significant changes.
