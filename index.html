Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2, 2025

Summary: We collect only the data necessary to provide account features and team collaboration. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Data Collection

To provide cloud synchronization and team features, "Reportify" collects the following information:

  • Account Information: Email address and Full Name (used for authentication and team identification).
  • User Content: Reports, photos, and signatures you explicitly upload are stored securely in the cloud to enable synchronization across your devices and team members.

2. Data Storage & Security

We use Google Firebase (Firestore & Cloud Storage) to store your data securely.

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest on Google's servers.
  • Strict security rules ensure that only you and your designated team members can access your reports.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely for:

  • Authenticating you into the app.
  • Synchronizing your reports across multiple devices.
  • Enabling "Team Sharing" features with your colleagues.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or third parties.

4. Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and all associated data (including reports and images) directly from within the app settings via the "Delete Account" button. This action is permanent.

5. Camera and Photo Library

"Reportify" requests access to your camera and photo library to allow you to add images to your reports. These images are uploaded to our secure cloud storage only when you choose to save or sync a report.
